# 06.15 — Write and test the model incident playbook

## Outcome

Document triage from alert to traces/logs/profiles/lineage/run, mitigation options, rollback, evidence preservation, and follow-up.

## Allowed paths

- `docs/runbooks/model-incident.md`
- `tests/runbooks/**`

## Deliverables

- Document triage from alert to traces/logs/profiles/lineage/run, mitigation options, rollback, evidence preservation, and follow-up.
- Add a tabletop checklist.

- A reviewer can identify affected model/data/deployment from a sample alert.
- Playbook distinguishes service outage from quality incident.

```bash
make test-runbooks
```
