# 02.01 — Define identities, roles, and permissions

## Outcome

Define human, service, CI, admin, and read-only personas across Kubernetes, registry, MLflow, storage, and future services.

## Allowed paths

- `docs/security/access-matrix.md`
- `adr/**`

## Deliverables

- Define human, service, CI, admin, and read-only personas across Kubernetes, registry, MLflow, storage, and future services.
- Mark every permission owner and approval boundary.

## Acceptance

- Matrix includes explicit deny cases and credential lifetimes.
- Reviewer approves the least-privilege baseline.

## Verify

```bash
rg -n 'Human|Service|CI|Deny|Lifetime' docs/security/access-matrix.md
```

## Non-goals

- Installing an identity provider.
