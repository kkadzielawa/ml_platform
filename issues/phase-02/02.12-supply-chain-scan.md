# 02.12 — Scan dependencies and images

## Outcome

Configure one pinned scanner for vulnerabilities/secrets and one license-policy check over the fixture.

## Allowed paths

- `config/trivy/**`
- `config/grype/**`
- `config/license-policy/**`
- `tests/supply-chain/policy/**`
- `docs/security/scan-policy.md`

- Configure one pinned scanner for vulnerabilities/secrets and one license-policy check over the fixture.

## Verify

```bash
make scan-fixture && make test-scan-policy
```

- Remediating third-party vulnerabilities or making legal judgments.
