# 10.05 — Implement deletion and tombstone semantics

## Outcome

Remove deleted content from retrieval, record auditable tombstones, and define physical purge/retention behavior.

## Allowed paths

- `src/ml_platform/rag/deletion/**`
- `tests/rag/deletion/**`
- `docs/rag/deletion.md`

## Deliverables

- Remove deleted content from retrieval, record auditable tombstones, and define physical purge/retention behavior.
- Propagate source deletion through chunk/index/cache references.

## Acceptance

- Deleted document is immediately unretrievable.
- Repeat deletion is idempotent and does not reveal prior content.

## Verify

```bash
make test-rag-deletion
```

## Non-goals

- Executing production legal deletion.
