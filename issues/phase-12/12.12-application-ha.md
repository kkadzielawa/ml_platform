# 12.12 — Add HA settings to selected stateless services

## Outcome

Configure replicas, anti-affinity/topology spread, disruption budgets, readiness, and rolling update for the selected facade/API services.

## Allowed paths

- `clusters/prod/**`
- `tests/resilience/applications/**`
- `docs/operations/application-ha.md`

## Deliverables

- Configure replicas, anti-affinity/topology spread, disruption budgets, readiness, and rolling update for the selected facade/API services.
- Test one pod and one node-scheduling disruption in disposable scope.

## Acceptance

- Service stays within study error budget.
- Misconfigured readiness fixture blocks rollout.

## Verify

```bash
make test-application-ha
```

## Non-goals

- Claiming HA for single-instance stateful services.
