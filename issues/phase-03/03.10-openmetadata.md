# 03.10 — Deploy and connect OpenMetadata

## Outcome

Deploy a pinned OpenMetadata OSS configuration sized for study use and connect identity.

## Allowed paths

- `platform/charts/openmetadata/**`
- `clusters/dev/openmetadata/**`
- `tests/integration/openmetadata/**`
- `docs/runbooks/openmetadata.md`

## Deliverables

- Deploy a pinned OpenMetadata OSS configuration sized for study use and connect identity.
- Register the baseline dataset, owner, schema, quality result, and lineage.

## Acceptance

- Asset is discoverable by name and owner.
- Lineage links raw input, curated output, and ingestion run.

## Verify

```bash
make apply-openmetadata && make test-openmetadata
```

## Non-goals

- Connecting every source or enabling enterprise-only features.
