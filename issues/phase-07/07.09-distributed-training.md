# 07.09 — Run a small distributed training job

## Outcome

Train a tiny deterministic model across at least two processes through the selected runtime and queue.

## Allowed paths

- `examples/distributed_training/**`
- `pipelines/distributed_training/**`
- `tests/integration/distributed_training/**`

## Deliverables

- Train a tiny deterministic model across at least two processes through the selected runtime and queue.
- Log topology, per-rank seed, metrics, artifacts, and utilization to MLflow.

## Acceptance

- Distributed result matches single-process baseline within declared tolerance.
- Job stays within assigned quota.

## Verify

```bash
make run-distributed-smoke && make test-distributed-result
```

## Non-goals

- Scaling to a large dataset/model.
