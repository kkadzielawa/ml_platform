# 07.11 — Exercise node or worker failure behavior

## Outcome

Inject a scoped worker/pod failure during the tiny job and observe retry/resume behavior.

## Allowed paths

- `tests/resilience/training/**`
- `scripts/faults/training/**`
- `docs/reports/training-failure.md`

## Deliverables

- Inject a scoped worker/pod failure during the tiny job and observe retry/resume behavior.
- Record lost work, restart time, and final correctness.

## Acceptance

- Failure injection targets only labeled fixture resources.
- Job either resumes correctly or fails terminally with actionable status.

## Verify

```bash
make test-training-failure
```

## Non-goals

- Physical node shutdown without authorization.
